Privacy Policy

This application is used exclusively for personal backup of Google Drive files using Rclone and Amazon S3.

Data Access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive files and their metadata.

Data Usage

Data is accessed only to perform backups initiated by the account owner.

Data Storage

Backup copies are transferred to a private Amazon S3 bucket controlled by the account owner.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Google Drive and Amazon Web Services process data as part of their respective services.

Access and Revocation

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account security settings and delete stored backups from Amazon S3.
